# Recipe correction plan

All corrections are local only. Nothing has been committed, deployed, or written to production. Recipe IDs (slugs) are unchanged, so saved meals, meal history and shared links keep working. Every recipe edit is itemized in `CHANGE-LOG.md` / `change-log.csv` (generated by `scripts/recipe-quality/corrections/apply.ts`).

Food-safety reference: USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart and FDA Food Code 3-501.16 — poultry 165°F; ground meat 160°F (ground poultry 165°F); whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb 145°F + 3-minute rest; fish 145°F or opaque and flaking; leftovers and reheated precooked meats 165°F; hot holding 140°F+.

## Remaining (proposed, in order)

1. **Doneness policy decision** — ~20 recipes target below-USDA doneness on purpose (medium-rare steak, tri-tip, lamb at 115–135°F; salmon at 125–130°F; pho beef cooked by broth). Awaiting decision before editing.
2. **B7 mechanical, batches of 10** — 77 recipes use an ingredient in the steps that isn't listed; 32 list an ingredient never used; 27 have scaling leftovers like "3.2 limes". Each needs a culinary call, not a find-and-replace.
3. **B8 nutrition** — 7 first-impression recipes now show "estimate coming soon" because their old values were implausible and the recalculation didn't pass plausibility checks (beef dip, jerk chicken, pulled pork, mac and cheese bake, loaded potato bar, peanut chicken bowls, steak tacos). Ingredient mapping fixes, then recalculation.
4. **Images** — biscuit French toast sliders hero shows a printed sign and staged set; needs a replacement image. Three more first-impression images are marked for a visual check.
5. **22 public-but-unapproved pages** — approve or retire after review.
