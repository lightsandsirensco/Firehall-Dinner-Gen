# Firehall Meals — SEO Growth Plan

**Role:** SEO Director strategy  
**Date:** June 22, 2026  
**North star:** Own organic search for **firefighter meal planning** — not generic recipe SEO.  
**12-month targets:** #1 for `firefighter meals`, `fire station meals`, `firehouse meals`.

---

## Executive summary

Firehall Meals has the strongest **on-page foundation** in the firefighter meal niche: 311 indexable recipes, 58 guides, 7 SEO landing pages, clean sitemap/robots, and Recipe + Article + FAQ schema. Technical audits pass (393 URLs, 0 canonical issues, 0 duplicate meta).

The gap is not “more recipes.” The gap is **topical authority packaging**, **pillar page dominance**, **internal link graph completion**, and **off-page signals** (backlinks, brand mentions, forum presence). Competitors like MyStationChef and The Station Chef compete on brand and community; AllRecipes and Serious Eats win on domain authority but ignore firefighter-specific intent.

**Priority order (next 90 days):**
1. Launch missing pillar URLs (`/firehouse-meals`, `/crew-meals`, `/firefighter-dinner-ideas`)
2. Reconcile homepage SEO (keyword-rich without killing personal-first conversion)
3. Merge duplicate guides; elevate 8 cornerstone guides to true pillar pages
4. Add recipe → guide contextual links (currently recipe-only clusters)
5. Add HowTo + Review + Speakable schema where appropriate
6. Search Console + backlink campaign (departments, training academies, fire service media)
