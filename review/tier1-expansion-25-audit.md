- **Patty melt / tuna melt / BLT / hall grilled cheese** — none  
- **French onion soup** — none  
- **Pasta e fagioli** — none  
- **Chicken tortilla soup** (brothy, lime, tortilla garnish) — none  
- **Italian-American wedding soup** — none  
- **New Orleans red beans & rice** — none (jambalaya ≠ red beans)  
- **Chicken piccata** (lemon-caper skillet) — none  
- **St. Louis–style barbecued ribs** (trim + oven finish) — ribs exist but not this **cut + method** positioning  
- **White-sauce grilled chicken pieces** (dip-style) — Alabama **pulled pork** exists; grilled **chicken** with white sauce does not  

### Duplicate rules applied

Rejected from consideration: second french onion (SE vs ATK), second patty melt (SE pork vs ATK beef), second baked oatmeal (SE vs ATK), second pasta e fagioli variant (smoked-paprika chicken vs 30-minute), NY strip reverse sear, CI jerk sheet pan (jerk chicken & rice), F&W pulled NC chicken sandwiches, lazy/spatchcock roast chicken, ATK roasted thighs + potatoes (herb thighs), CI sheet-pan wings (smoked BBQ wings tray).
