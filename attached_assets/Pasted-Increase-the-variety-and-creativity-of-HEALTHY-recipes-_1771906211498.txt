Increase the variety and creativity of HEALTHY recipes in the Firehall Meals generator while keeping them realistic for a crew.

Goals:
- More healthy options than comfort options
- Much higher variety (avoid repeats)
- Healthy meals must still feel hearty and satisfying for firefighters

Implementation requirements:

1) Healthy bias
- When “Healthy” is selected, produce a healthy recipe 100% of the time.
- When “Any” is selected, bias results toward healthy ~70% of the time.

2) Healthy variety rules (hard constraints)
- Maintain a “recent recipes memory” of the last 15 generated meals (title + primary protein + cuisine + cooking method + key ingredients).
- Do not repeat the same:
  - cuisine category within last 3
  - cooking method within last 3
  - primary protein within last 2
  - base carb (rice/pasta/potato/tortilla) within last 2
- Rotate across at least these cuisine profiles:
  Mediterranean, Mexican-inspired, Korean-inspired, Thai-inspired, Indian-inspired, Japanese-inspired, Middle Eastern, Italian-inspired (lighter), BBQ/Smoky (lean), Canadian comfort-lite.

3) Healthy meals must include at least 2 of these “quality signals”
- 30g+ protein per serving (or clearly “high protein”)
- 1+ high-fiber ingredient (beans/lentils/chickpeas/veg/whole grains)
- Healthy fat source (olive oil/avocado/nuts/seeds)
- Veg volume (2+ cups veg per serving OR 3+ veg ingredients)
- Lower added sugar (no sweet sauces unless low-sugar alternative)

4) Avoid “boring healthy”
- Do NOT default to plain chicken + broccoli + rice.
- Avoid repetitive “salad” meals unless user selects salad explicitly.
- Use bold but healthy flavor builders:
  citrus, vinegars, herbs, garlic, ginger, spice blends, yogurt sauces, salsa verde, chimichurri, gochujang-style but lower sugar, miso, tahini-lemon, etc.

5) Station practicality
- Keep total time within the selected time filter.
- Prefer one-pan, sheet-pan, slow cooker, or pot meals when “busy hall” is selected.
- Provide prep shortcuts (pre-chopped veg, frozen veg, rotisserie chicken if allowed).

6) Output tags
- Add tags to each meal:
  - cuisine
  - cooking method
  - “high protein”
  - “high fiber” (if applicable)
  - “quick cleanup”
This helps enforce variety rules.

7) Keep existing features intact
- Do not remove filters or change email/print/shopping list.
- Keep recipe format and JSON schema the same.

Acceptance test:
- Generate 20 healthy meals in a row and confirm they are meaningfully different in cuisine, method, and ingredients.
