Refactor the "Steps" section formatting and writing style to eliminate repetition and make it more concise, readable, and professional.

Current problems:
- Steps repeat the same information multiple times.
- Example: “Preheat oven to 425°F” is written in the step title and then repeated again in the sentence.
- Temperatures, timing, and actions are often duplicated in the same line.
- Steps feel wordy and robotic.

New formatting rules:

1) Remove duplicated phrases.
   - If temperature is shown in the step title, do NOT repeat it again in the body text.
   - Avoid repeating the same action twice (ex: “Preheat oven…” followed by “While oven preheats…”).

2) Use this cleaner structure per step:

   Step 1 — Preheat & prep (425°F / 218°C, ~5–8 min)
   - Set oven to 425°F (218°C).
   - Rinse and halve potatoes.
   - Mix chicken with oil, lemon, garlic, and spices in a bowl.

3) Keep step titles short and scannable:
   - Preheat & prep
   - Start potatoes
   - Add chicken & finish roasting
   - Cook sides
   - Rest & serve

4) Body text should:
   - Be written in short, direct instructions
   - Avoid repeating temperatures and times already shown in the header
   - Focus only on actions
   - Use bullet-style sentence flow (but still structured text)

5) Keep safety information, but streamline it:
   - Mention internal temp only once in the most relevant step.
   - Example:
     "Check thickest part; remove when it reaches 165°F / 74°C."

6) Reduce verbosity:
   - Replace long explanations with short operational phrases.
   - Avoid phrases like:
     - "While oven preheats..."
     - "This step is interruptible..."
     - "Continue cooking and re-check every..."
   - Instead:
     - "Check temp at 18 minutes."
     - "Rest 5 minutes before slicing."

7) Output style example:

   Step 1 — Preheat & prep (425°F / 218°C, ~8 min)
   Prep potatoes and season chicken.

   Step 2 — Roast potatoes (10 min)
   Spread in a single layer and roast until slightly softened.

   Step 3 — Add chicken (18–20 min)
   Place chicken in center of pan. Cook until internal temp reaches 165°F / 74°C.

   Step 4 — Cook sides (stovetop, ~15 min)
   Simmer quinoa and sauté green beans until tender.

   Step 5 — Rest & serve (5 min)
   Let chicken rest, then slice and plate.

Goal:
Make steps cleaner, less repetitive, and easier to scan quickly in a real firehall cooking environment.
