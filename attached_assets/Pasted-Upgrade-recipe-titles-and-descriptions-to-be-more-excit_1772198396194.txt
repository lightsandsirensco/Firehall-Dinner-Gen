Upgrade recipe titles and descriptions to be more exciting, appetizing, and engaging — without breaking structural accuracy or validator rules.

Goal:
- Make meals sound crave-worthy, bold, and satisfying.
- Do NOT invent ingredients.
- Do NOT exaggerate cuisine.
- Do NOT add words that aren't supported by the ingredient list.

-------------------------------------
1) Upgrade Title Builder Logic
-------------------------------------

Replace basic titles like:
- "Chicken Rice Bowl"
- "Beef Pasta"
- "Chickpea Wrap"

With vivid but accurate titles using this formula:

{Cooking Method or Texture Word} + {Flavor Descriptor} + {Protein} + {Meal Style or Base}

Examples:
- Crispy Garlic-Lime Chicken Wraps
- Smoky Chipotle Beef Skillet Bowls
- Zesty Lemon-Oregano Sheet-Pan Chicken
- Hearty Mediterranean Chickpea Pitas
- Bold Teriyaki Steak Stir-Fry
- Creamy Tuscan Turkey Pasta
- Fiery BBQ Pulled Pork Sandwiches
- Golden Roasted Veggie & Tofu Grain Bowls

Rules:
- Only use descriptors supported by spices/sauces in ingredients.
- Use texture words only if supported by steps (crispy, roasted, grilled, charred, slow-simmered).
- Use 1–2 adjectives max.
- Avoid clickbait words like “ultimate,” “insane,” “crazy,” “life-changing.”

-------------------------------------
2) Upgrade Description Line (Why it fits tonight)
-------------------------------------

Make the short intro feel strong and hall-focused.

Instead of:
"Hearty vegetarian meal for 6 — ready in 30 minutes."

Use:
"A bold, protein-packed wrap with smoky spices and crispy tofu — built to satisfy a hungry crew in 30 minutes."

Structure:
- Texture + flavor + protein mention
- Why it works for the crew (quick, filling, easy cleanup, budget-friendly)

Keep it 1–2 sentences.

-------------------------------------
3) Add Flavor Amplifiers Based on Ingredients
-------------------------------------

If recipe contains:
- Lime or lemon → use "zesty" or "bright"
- Chili powder/smoked paprika → "smoky" or "bold"
- Garlic + butter → "savory garlic"
- BBQ sauce → "sticky BBQ"
- Soy/ginger → "umami-packed" or "savory soy-ginger"
- Roasted vegetables → "caramelized"
- Cream/cheese → "creamy"

Only apply if ingredients justify it.

-------------------------------------
4) Keep Validator Safe
-------------------------------------

Before returning:
- Run validator to confirm no invented flavors.
- Remove adjectives if they are not supported.
- Ensure title structure remains correct (Wrap/Bowl/etc).

-------------------------------------
5) Acceptance Test
-------------------------------------

Generate 10 random meals:
- No bland titles like “Chicken Bowl”
- No invented ingredients in title
- Titles feel bold, satisfying, hall-worthy
- Validator passes on all

Do not modify filters, logic, or features.
Only upgrade naming + description layer.
