Improve the recipe photos on the Explore page so they look much more enticing, appetizing, and high quality.

Goal:
Make the Explore page feel more visually crave-worthy and premium by using better food images that make users want to click and cook the recipes.

Requirements:

1) Improve image quality
- Use higher-quality, more appetizing recipe photos where available
- Prefer images that clearly show the finished dish
- Avoid dull, blurry, poorly cropped, or low-light images
- Avoid photos that look generic, flat, or unprofessional

2) Prioritize images that feel:
- warm
- rich in color
- well-lit
- close enough to show texture
- realistic and satisfying
- suitable for firefighter-style meals (hearty, filling, bold)

3) Improve image presentation in the UI
- Use consistent aspect ratios across all Explore cards
- Ensure images are cropped cleanly and do not cut off the main food awkwardly
- Add subtle rounded corners and card polish to make the images feel premium
- Make sure images scale cleanly on mobile

4) Add a visual enhancement layer if needed
- Slightly improve contrast / sharpness / saturation in a tasteful way
- Do not over-edit or make the food look fake
- Keep the same theme and premium dark UI

5) Improve recipe card hierarchy
- Make the image the strongest visual anchor of each Explore card
- Ensure title, tags, and metadata sit nicely below the image
- Add subtle hover/tap polish so cards feel more clickable

6) If multiple image sources are available:
- Prefer the most appetizing one
- Avoid repetitive stock-like images
- If a recipe image is weak, allow fallback to a better-quality relevant food image if possible

7) Maintain consistency
- All Explore images should feel like they belong in the same app
- Avoid some images feeling vibrant while others feel dull and lifeless

8) Do NOT:
- change the overall app theme
- make the photos cartoonish or AI-looking
- stretch or distort images
- break card loading performance unnecessarily

Final goal:
Make the Explore page images significantly more mouth-watering, polished, and engaging so users are more likely to click into recipes and come back to browse.
